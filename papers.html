    Co-authorConference posterOct 30, 2026

    Lower thalamic creatine and glutathione associate with cognition and systemic inflammation in neurological post-acute sequelae of COVID-19 infection from the multicenter COVID-BRAIN imaging cohort

    Morsal Mubarak, co-author (2nd author on the manuscript) · with the multicenter COVID-BRAIN study team

    Poster · Clinical Research Day 2026, Mass General Brigham · Athinoula A. Martinos Center for Biomedical Imaging, Massachusetts General Hospital

    A multicenter brain-imaging study of people with neurological Long COVID (neuroPASC). Using MR spectroscopy, an MRI scan that measures brain chemistry, the team found lower glutathione (an antioxidant) and creatine (an energy buffer) in the thalamus than in controls. 176 participants were analyzed (102 neuroPASC, 74 controls).

  2. First & corresponding authorManuscript2026

    PoxHostAtlas: A reproducible cross-study atlas of host gene-expression remodeling during poxvirus infection

    Morsal Mubarak (Justice High School) and Deon Nguyen (Arizona State University)

    Manuscript · 21 pages · open code on GitHub

    Re-analyzes public poxvirus RNA-sequencing data (two vaccinia infections and two experiments expressing a single myxoma-virus protein) with one common workflow to test which host-cell responses repeat across studies. Across four datasets, 1,834 host genes changed reproducibly (871 up, 963 down).

  3. Sole authorUnder review · Scientific ReportsAug 23, 2026

    A Reproducible Public-Data Benchmark of Birthweight-for-Gestational-Age Deviations and Infant/Fetal Mortality Risk: A Cross-Sectional Ecological Analysis of United States Vital Statistics, 2016–2024

    Morsal Mubarak · independent research project

    Under review at Scientific Reports · 20 pages · code and data archived on Zenodo

    Turns 33,589,282 U.S. birth records (National Center for Health Statistics public-use files, 2016–2024) into an open benchmark that anyone can re-run to test measures of fetal growth against infant and fetal death. Below the 10th percentile of birthweight for gestational age, the infant-death rate was 3.4 times, and the fetal-death (stillbirth) rate 5.2 times, the rate in the 10th–90th percentile range.

    Academic Pressure and Mental Health in South Asian American High School Students

    M. Mubarak, A. Qadir, H. Mangat, M. Ramirez, M. Raza, P. Ly, S. Sridhar

    Bay Area Medical Explorers · Mental Health Research final paper · 12 pages

    A survey of South Asian and South-East Asian high-school students about academic pressure, stress, coping, and asking for help. 93% of respondents reported regular stress-related mental-health concerns, yet 89% said they had never sought professional help because they felt they did not need it.

  5. Report
    Lead Computational ResearcherUnpublished analysisSpring 2026

    Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI) MRI Computational Study

    Morsal Mubarak, Lead Computational Researcher

    Verified analysis report for the study's principal investigator · Mass General Brigham / MGH Martinos Center

    A Python pipeline (about 4,800 lines) that tests whether brain chemistry measured by MR spectroscopy changes with treatment after traumatic brain injury and whether it tracks post-concussion symptoms: 35 participants with spectroscopy, four timepoints, outlier screening, multiple-comparison correction, and a full re-run audit.

  6. Co-first authorResearch paperMar 17, 2026

    Assessing the Associations Between Antiretroviral Treatment Resource Shortage and HIV Prevalence Among Teenagers Residing in Zambia

    C. Cimarusti and M. Mubarak

    Bay Area Medical Explorers · 9 pages

    Uses Zambia Demographic and Health Survey data (2018–2024) and published studies to ask whether shortages of medical resources, including antiretroviral therapy (ART, the daily HIV medicine), relate to HIV among adolescents aged 15–19. The paper reports that resource shortages were significantly correlated with lower treatment access and higher HIV prevalence.

    Nourish to Flourish: Empowering Communities through Food Education and EBT Access

    Morsal Mubarak · PHYAP project

    Policy proposal deck · Public Health Youth Ambassador Program (PHYAP), Fairfax County Health Department · 15 slides

    2nd place (solo) of 17 finalists, 2025 PHYAP Community Health Competition final (Georgetown University School of Medicine, Department of Family Medicine)

    A plan to close the food-access gap for students after high school: a one-week after-school workshop in the last month of school where graduating students apply for an EBT card (the card used for SNAP food benefits), practice telling needs from wants, find at least two food pantries near them, and learn to eat healthy on a budget from a nutritionist.
