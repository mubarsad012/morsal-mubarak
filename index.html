Lead computational researcher for a 35-participant study of brain chemistry after traumatic brain injury. PoxHostAtlas: Host Gene-Expression Remodeling During Poxvirus Infection

A cross-study atlas that re-analyzes public RNA-seq datasets to test which host-cell responses to poxviruses (including vaccinia) are reproducible, using meta-analysis, co-expression networks, and machine-learning stress tests.

Explore the atlas PDF
1,834host genes reproducibly changed across datasets

Brain Metabolism in Long COVID (COVID-BRAIN)

Third author on a poster from the multicenter COVID-BRAIN imaging study, which found lower thalamic glutathione and creatine in people with neurological Long COVID, linked to cognition and inflammation. Presented at Mass General Brigham Clinical Research Day 2026.

Section III · Community Impact

Family medical & legal interpreter · English ⇄ Pashto

Why I build tools that translate.

As one of the main fluent English speakers in my household, I interpret between English and Pashto at medical appointments and with legal paperwork, for my family and for refugee community members. Those conversations are why ProxyHealth AI exists: every family deserves to understand the documents that decide their care. Today it explains papers in 27 languages, including Pashto and Dari.

Section IV · About / CV

About me

I'm a student at Justice High School in Falls Church, Virginia. I teach myself to code by building things I wish already existed: a plain-language guide to the paperwork my family and neighbors receive, and an open benchmark anyone can re-run on 33.5 million U.S. birth records.

I care about research that other people can check. That's why my fetal-growth benchmark and PoxHostAtlas ship with public code, data manifests, and file hashes, and why I explain results in language a family, not just a reviewer, can follow.
